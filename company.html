Corporate operating model

One company record controls requests, approvals, billing, and portal access.

Instead of handling every request like a new enquiry, Kridiya links approved companies to a corporate account. That makes repeat travel cleaner: your request history, contacts, billing rules, documents, and monthly reporting stay connected.
