Portal governance

Access is useful because it is controlled.

Corporate portal access is not a public self-signup. Kridiya reviews the company, creates the account, links the approved contact, then issues login details. This protects company data and keeps supplier pricing/internal notes separate.

ApplyCompany submits account request and contact details.
ReviewKridiya checks company, services, billing, and approval process.
ActivatePortal user is linked to one approved corporate account.
OperateRequests, quotes, bookings, documents, and statements stay connected.
