Operating flow

Use this process for every company travel request.

  1. RequestCompany submits route, travellers, dates, payment/LPO needs, and approval person.
  2. QuoteKridiya checks suppliers and sends options with rules, deadline, and total cost.
  3. ApprovalCompany confirms written approval, payment, credit terms, or LPO before booking.
  4. BookingKridiya creates the booking, tracks supplier cost, and prepares documents.
  5. HandoverInvoice, receipt, ticket, voucher, visa note, or refund/cancellation update is shared.
