Corporate access

Apply once. We review your company and create your corporate access.

Do not create usernames publicly. The secure flow is: company applies, Kridiya reviews the details, Kridiya approves the company account, then username/password is issued to the authorized contact.

After submit Kridiya will contact you and issue username/password after approval.
